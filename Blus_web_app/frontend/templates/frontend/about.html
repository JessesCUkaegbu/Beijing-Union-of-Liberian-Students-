Who We Are

BLUS is a community-driven platform built to support Liberian students in Beijing — helping them navigate university life, stay connected, and access the resources they need.

Our Purpose

Built by students,
for students

We started as a small association with one goal: make student life less overwhelming. Today, BLUS connects hundreds of Liberian students across universities in Beijing, offering tools for community, finance, and support.

Whether you're new to the country or a long-time member, we're here to make sure no student feels alone.
